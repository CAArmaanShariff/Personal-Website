About Me

I consider myself an eternal optimist. I strongly believe in the adage that it is the greatest injustice to us if we give ourselves anything less than the best.

I’m a CA by designation but I’m so much more than that – I’m a writer, a teacher, an author, a dancer (especially SRK songs) and a very bad singer! We were not meant to be typecasted to a single box – we were meant for more things on this beautiful earth! I love all things art and live by Oscar Wilde’s quote ‘Art for Art’s sake’. I consider myself a high agency person who goes after what he wants and who also indulges in various side quests!

I’ve always been fascinated by the field of economics & public policy and I hope to be a part of that discipline very soon. I love academics and always found a deep joy in learning new things in whichever professional endeavour I’ve undertaken.

There is so much out there in this world that we have not experienced yet and I truly believe that we owe it to ourselves to live with whimsy & joy & to live life to the fullest!

Career trajectory

  1. Finance Transformation Consultant

    Deloitte India

    Always dreamt of living the management consultant’s life and it has finally arrived. I’m in the midst of great company and very smart and nice people in this firm. Looking forward to the experiences and learnings!

  2. Incoming Fellow

    YLAC

    Got selected for the YLAC – Policy in Action Fellowship on a partial scholarship. My long-term vision has always been to work in public policy and this is a step in that direction. Super excited for this!

  3. Adjunct Professor

    Christ University, Bangalore

    I found my love for teaching when I was around 16 years old. In that process, I realised that I understood the subject much better when I taught it to someone. When the concept clicks in my student’s mind like an ‘A-hah’ moment, it is the most gratifying feeling and therefore I chased that feeling by taking up a stint at my alma mater. I had the privilege of teaching and inspiring the sweetest and quite bright students in Christ. It was truly a memorable experience for me!

  4. Auditor

    KPMG India

    My first corporate exposure that moulded me to become the well rounded professional that I am today. Lots of memories made over late nights and early mornings of intense work. Many people were my first mentors there from whom I learnt so much and I still continue to take those learnings with me wherever I go. Ever grateful.
